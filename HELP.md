# PawRecord Help

PawRecord keeps your pet's health records, vaccinations and vet appointments in one place. This page explains how to use it, whether you're a pet owner, a veterinarian or a clinic admin.

---

## Getting started

### Signing in
1. Open PawRecord and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter the username and password the clinic gave you.
3. You'll be taken to your home page. What you see there depends on your role: pet owner, vet or admin.

PawRecord accounts are created by the clinic, so there's no sign-up page. If you don't have an account or forgot your password, contact the clinic.

### Installing the app
PawRecord can be installed on your phone or computer like a regular app:
- On the landing page, select **Install app**.
- Or, in your browser's menu, choose **Install PawRecord** (Chrome, Edge) or **Add to Home Screen** (Safari on iPhone).

Once installed, PawRecord opens in its own window and keeps working when you're offline.

### Signing out
Select **Sign out** at the bottom of the sidebar. If you share a device with others, always sign out when you're done.

## For pet owners

### Adding a pet
1. Go to **My Pets** and select **Add pet**.
2. Fill in your pet's name, species, breed and birthdate.
3. Select **Save**.

You can edit or delete your pets at any time from their profile. Deleting a pet asks you to confirm first.

### Booking an appointment
1. Go to **Appointments** and select **Book appointment**.
2. Choose your pet, a date and an available time slot (8:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
3. Add a short reason for the visit, then select **Book**.

Your booking starts as **Pending** until the clinic reviews it. You'll get a notification when it's approved, declined or moved to a different time.

### Cancelling an appointment
Open the appointment and select **Cancel**. To change the date or time, cancel and book again, or contact the clinic.

### Viewing your pet's records
Open a pet from **My Pets** to see its profile: age, last visit, next booster date and every record the vet has logged, such as vaccinations, treatments and checkups.

## For veterinarians

### Your schedule
**Appointments** lists your confirmed appointments. Select one to see its details, then:
- **Log a record** to record the visit. The appointment is marked **Completed**.
- **View pet profile** to see the pet's full history.

New booking requests are handled by the clinic admin. Once an appointment is confirmed and assigned to you, it appears on your schedule.

### Finding a pet
Go to **Pets** to see every pet in the clinic. Select a pet to see a quick preview, or double-click it to open its full profile.

### Logging a record
1. Open a pet's profile (or an appointment) and select **Log a record**.
2. Choose the record type (vaccination, treatment or checkup) and the medication given.
3. Take photos of the pet and the vaccine vial with your device's camera.
4. Set the next booster date, if there is one, then select **Save**.

The owner is notified when a new record is saved. You can also log records for walk-in pets that don't have an appointment.

### Editing a record
Open the pet's profile, find the record and select **Edit**. You can only edit records you logged yourself.

---

## For clinic admins

### Reviewing bookings
New bookings appear as **Pending**. For each one you can:
- **Approve** it. The appointment becomes **Confirmed**.
- **Decline** it. The owner can book again.
- **Reschedule** it to a new date and time. It's confirmed right away.
- **Assign a vet** to it.

The owner is notified every time an appointment's status changes.

### Managing pets and records
Admins can edit and delete any pet, and delete records from the record details window. Deleting always asks you to confirm first.

## Appointment statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Booked by the owner, waiting for the clinic to review it |
| **Confirmed** | Approved (or rescheduled) by the clinic and assigned to a vet |
| **Completed** | The visit happened and the vet logged a record |
| **Declined** | The clinic couldn't accept the booking |
| **Cancelled** | The owner cancelled the booking |

---

## Notifications

Pet owners get notified when:

| Notification | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| Booking update | The clinic approves, declines or reschedules your booking |
| Upcoming visit | The day before a confirmed appointment |
| New record | A vet logs a record for your pet |
| Booster due | 3 days before your pet's next booster date, and again the day after if it's missed |

---

## Booster statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Up to date** | The next booster is more than 3 days away |
| **Due soon** | The next booster is within the next 3 days |
| **Overdue** | The booster date has passed; book an appointment soon |

---

## Using PawRecord offline

PawRecord works without an internet connection after you've signed in once on that device:
- Pages and records you've already opened stay available.
- Vets can log records while offline. They're saved on the device and sent to the clinic automatically once you're back online.
- Some actions, like signing in or booking an appointment, need an internet connection.

## Troubleshooting

**I can't sign in.**
Check your username and password, including capital letters. If it still doesn't work, ask the clinic to check your account.

**I'm sent back to the sign-in page.**
Your session has expired. Sign in again to continue.

**The page shows old information.**
Make sure you're online, then refresh the page. If you're offline, PawRecord shows the last saved version until you reconnect.

**My pet's age or birthdate looks wrong.**
Edit the pet's profile and check the birthdate.

**I still need help.**
Contact the clinic directly.
